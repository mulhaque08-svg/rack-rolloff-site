Dumpster Rental FAQs

Frequently asked questions about ordering, weight limits, and delivery conditions.

How long can I keep the dumpster container?

Our standard residential rental period is 7 days. You can choose to extend your rental when booking for an additional $15 per day if your construction or cleanout takes longer.

What happens if I exceed the weight capacity?

Each container size includes a designated weight allowance (e.g., 3 tons for a 20-yard dumpster). If your load exceeds this, the sorting facility assesses an overage fee of $75/ton, which is charged transparently.

Do I need a permit to place the dumpster on the street?

If the dumpster is placed on private property (like your driveway), no permit is needed. If you require placing it on a public street or easement, the City of Houston requires a Right-of-Way Permit. Let us know and we can assist you with the request!
